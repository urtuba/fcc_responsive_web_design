Phineas Taylor Barnum

Phineas Taylor Barnum (1810–1891) was an American businessman, showman and entertainer. He lived in New York City, where he founded two museums and a circus. He was good at being the center of attention, and he is known as the first show business millionaire. He said, "I am a showman by profession", but for some time he was also interested in writing, publishing and politics.
